Social media is a numbers game - and first impressions happen fast.

People naturally use visible signals to understand what's happening around them.

A post with 3 likes feels different from a post with 3,000.

A profile with 80 followers feels different from one with 8,000.

These numbers don't prove that content is good — but they can change how people perceive its momentum and whether they stop, look, interact, or keep scrolling.

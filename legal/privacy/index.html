Privacy

We store the email, public target, quantity, and comments you submit so we can fulfil and support the order. Stripe processes the card. We do not collect social-media passwords.

Capability tokens stay in your browser session and are stored on the server only as a hash. We do not put them in URLs, emails, or logs.

Order records are retained for support and accounting, then reduced according to the retention schedule. Contact support@like-dealer.com for access or deletion requests.
